# Cinematic Industrial Editorial

A reusable style reference derived from the supplied founder portrait and sunrise construction photograph.

## Style Summary

Premium commercial photography with grounded documentary realism. The imagery feels confident, capable, warm, and quietly aspirational—polished without looking staged or glossy.

## Core Visual Language

### Lighting

- Low, directional golden-hour light from the side or rear
- Warm amber highlights with soft natural falloff
- Deep, detailed shadows and controlled contrast
- Subtle rim or edge light separating subjects from their surroundings
- Natural-looking illumination rather than obvious studio lighting

### Color Grade

- Warm browns, bronze, ochre, muted gold, and near-black
- Restrained slate, steel blue, or dark navy as a cool counterpoint
- Slightly desaturated overall
- Rich blacks without losing important shadow detail
- Protected highlights with a gentle filmic roll-off

### Composition

- Wide 16:9 framing
- Strong off-center subject placement
- Meaningful environmental context
- Generous negative space
- Clear structural, architectural, or directional lines
- Balanced visual weight without rigid symmetry

### Realism and Texture

- Photorealistic, tactile, and believable
- Preserve natural skin texture and subtle imperfections
- Show fabric weave, wood grain, concrete, dust, glass, and metal accurately
- Crisp primary subject with dimensional separation
- Subtle film grain; no synthetic smoothness

### Mood

- Thoughtful
- Purposeful
- Resilient
- Established
- Quietly optimistic
- Authentic and unstaged

### Focus and Lens Feel

- Portraits: approximately 50–85mm perspective with soft background separation
- Environments: approximately 24–35mm perspective with moderate-to-deep depth of field
- Natural perspective without extreme wide-angle distortion
- Sharp focal subject with smooth, cinematic depth transitions

## Consistency Checklist

Before accepting a generated image, confirm that it has:

- Warm directional light balanced by cool environmental tones
- Deep but readable shadows
- An off-center subject with clear environmental context
- Natural materials and believable surface texture
- Restrained saturation and subtle filmic finishing
- A candid, purposeful mood
- No glossy stock-photo styling or exaggerated effects
