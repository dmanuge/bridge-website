Bridge.co redesign · design direction study

Ten directions for one homepage

Bridge finances two very different borrowers: consumer brands funding purchase orders and production, and hotel owners financing commercial real estate. The hard design problem is speaking to both in the first five seconds without splitting the brand in two. Each direction below pairs a different answer to that problem with a distinct visual language, so you can judge the strategy and the look separately.

All ten stay inside the current brand system (Bridge Blue, Deep Navy, Off-White, the arch mark) but push it to different places. Headlines are written for real, not lorem. Stats come from current brand materials and deal figures are illustrative, so verify both before anything ships.

01 to 04: route the visitor 05 to 07: earn trust first 08 to 10: lead with product
Direction 01 · route the visitor · the fork

Split decision

Two doors, zero ambiguity. The homepage is the fork itself: brands go left, property owners go right, and the arch badge at the seam says one platform stands behind both.

Two-borrower strategy
Hard fork. Visitors self-select in the first second; nothing above the fold tries to serve both at once.
Visual language
Off-White vs Deep Navy panels, oversized grotesque headlines, line-art motifs, brand blue footer bar.
Inspiration
Kiavi's audience clarity, classic two-door agency sites, Drip Capital's segmented entry.
Choose this if
The two funnels stay truly separate and you want maximum routing efficiency from paid traffic.

Direction 02 · route the visitor · persona toggle

Inspiration

Direction 05 · earn trust first · quiet authority

The institution

An asset-manager posture instead of a startup one. Serif type, a stat ledger, hairline rules and almost no decoration. The two businesses appear as capabilities of one credit desk, not as products competing for attention.

Two-borrower strategy
Both as capabilities. The page sells underwriting discipline; each borrower finds their mandate in a two-column ledger.
Visual language
Deep Navy field, Source Serif display, mono microtype, Bridge Green reserved for a few quiet marks.
Inspiration
Setpoint's infrastructure tone, institutional credit shops, annual report covers.

Deal sizes are growing and the site's job is to make a $50M sponsor and their lender both feel at home.

Direction 07 · earn trust first · numbers first

Terms on the table

Two-borrower strategy
Side-by-side spec cards. Each audience reads their own column; the shared fine print carries the brand promise.
Visual language
White, hairline tables, tabular mono numerals, one charcoal CTA band. Green appears exactly once, on the 48 hours.
Inspiration
Aven and Figure's rate-forward honesty, Ramp's pricing clarity, term sheet typography.
Choose this if
Visitors arrive comparison shopping and you're confident Bridge wins on speed, cost and process.

Where this goes next

Picking a direction

These aren't ten finalists, they're ten arguments. The strategies mix and match: 03's umbrella brand takes 08's intake chips as its hero CTA nicely, and 09's tombstones would strengthen almost any direction as a section. The visual languages don't mix; pick one voice.

Before anything ships: verify the stats and illustrative deal terms with the capital markets team, confirm Mabry Pro web licensing (these mockups embed open-license stand-ins), and budget photography if 06 or 09 is in the running.
